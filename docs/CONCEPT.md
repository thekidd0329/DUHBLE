# Dormant but Stateful Persistence

## High-level concept

Dormant but stateful persistence treats inactivity as distinct from absence.

A computational element may stop producing observable activity while retaining enough structure to preserve continuity across time. That retained structure may include identity, history, relationships, contextual associations, and readiness to become relevant again.

The central principle is:

> Something can stop acting without stopping existing as state.

This creates a design space between continuous activation and complete erasure. It allows a system to preserve latent structure across quiet periods and later continue from what already existed rather than reconstructing everything from scratch.

DUHBLE is the working language/runtime identity for exploring that design space.

## Boundaries

This concept alone does not establish intention, subjective experience, motivation, biological equivalence, or any other higher-level interpretation. Those are separate questions and require separate evidence.
