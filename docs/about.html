DUHBLE

State is not storage. Relationships are not metadata.

DUHBLE is an experimental stateful compute substrate intended to make meaningful state, causal history, relationships, provenance, and continuity part of the system itself.

THE DIFFERENCE

Describe what exists, what relates, and what makes change lawful.

Traditional languages primarily tell a machine what operations to perform. DUHBLE is being designed around a different center of gravity: what state exists, how pieces of state relate, what conditions make activity eligible, and what consequences survive afterward.

C++ describes instructions. DUHBLE describes state, relationships, and the conditions under which computation becomes necessary.

The host can mount, expose, reason over, and return information, but it should not become a second source of semantic truth.

01

Cause becomes memory.

An event becomes memory because a lawful event changed mounted state, not because an application stuffed another row into storage.

02

Relationships become computation.

Trust, hostility, association, eligibility, affinity, reinforcement, inhibition, and decay can live in the model and shape what happens next.

03

Continuity stops being theater.

A machine can leave, return, reconnect, or move between hosts while preserving owned state instead of rebuilding identity from transcripts.

04

Provenance becomes law.

Source, path, timing, eligibility, and state determine whether a change may become part of the machine.

ADD IT TO AI

Let the model generate language. Let DUHBLE carry continuity underneath it.

An LM or LLM can plug into DUHBLE as a reasoning or language surface while DUHBLE carries relationships, prior causes, learned associations, persistent state, permissions, and what actually changed over time.

Stop spending the whole context window reminding the machine who it was five minutes ago.

What we are not claiming

This is not a claim that DUHBLE is already a finished general-purpose replacement for C++, nor a claim of general intelligence. The architecture is being developed and tested as a new compute model with deliberately strict rules around state and change.
