# Frozen Checkpoint

**Checkpoint date:** August 26, 2026

The research state that preceded DUHBLE's naming is frozen.

Until the freeze is explicitly lifted:

- no new experiments are to be run from the frozen checkpoint;
- no tuning or modification of the frozen architecture is to be performed;
- no implementation details are to be published in this repository;
- public/high-level descriptions should emphasize the idea rather than the implementation.

The preserved idea is dormant-but-stateful persistence: inactive information can remain structurally present and recoverable rather than being erased or treated as zero.
